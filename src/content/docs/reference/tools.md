Free web calculators for the design math on this wiki. Always sanity-check a
calculator's output against a hand estimate before you cut metal.

## Part selection

- **[Ember Drivetrain Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/drivetrain)** —
  Wheel speed, gear ratio, and drivetrain performance. See
  [Power, torque & gearing](/fundamentals/power-torque-gearing/).
- **[Team Run Amok Tentacle Calculator](https://runamok.tech/squid/newtorquecalc.htm)** —
  Long-standing drivetrain / amp-hour model: pushing-match current and pack
  capacity from motors, voltage, gearing, wheel size, and robot weight.
- **[Ember Weapon Tip Speed Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/weapon)**
  and **[Bristol Bot Builders Tip Speed Calculator](https://bristolbotbuilders.com/tools/tipspeed/)** —
  Spinner RPM and tip speed. See [Weapon geometry & bite](/weapons/geometry-bite/).
- **[Ember Robot Builder](https://ember.level5robotics.com/builder)** —
  Step-by-step design of a complete robot powertrain.

## Mechanical design

- **[Ember Center Distance Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/center-distance)**
  and **[WCP Belt Calculator](https://wcproducts.com/pages/calculator-belt)** —
  Pulley centre-to-centre distance for a belt profile and tooth counts. See
  [Weapon motors & ESCs](/weapons/motors-escs/#belt-drive-vs-hubmotor).
- **[Ember Scale Factors](https://ember.level5robotics.com/scale-factors)** —
  Convert linear dimensions between weight classes. See
  [Weight budgeting](/fundamentals/weight-budgeting/).

## Electrical

- **[Ember Battery Size Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/battery-size)** —
  Pack capacity from current draw, discharge rate, and runtime. See
  [LiPo & LiHV batteries](/power/lipo-batteries/).
- **[Ember Watt-Hour Equivalency](https://ember.level5robotics.com/watt-hour)** —
  Equivalent capacities when changing cell count or voltage.
- **[Ember Motor Torque Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/torque)** _(beta)_ —
  Motor torque, output torque, and linear force from motor specs.

## Safety

- **[Ember Audio Tach Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/tach)** —
  Estimate weapon tip speed from an audio recording to check rule compliance.

## Strategy

- **[Ember Tournament Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/tournament)** —
  Fight counts and RCL point distribution for a bracket.

## Guides & references

- **Beetleweights for Dummies** (Leo Ping, with Lucas Skotiniotis, Ian Talbert
  & Derek Tran; Fall 2025) — a current, builder-maintained beginner guide to
  3&nbsp;lb robots, shared through the [NHRL Discord](https://wiki.nhrl.io/).
  Much of this wiki's North-American beetleweight practice is cross-checked
  against it.
- **[RioBotz Combat Robot Tutorial](https://www.riobotz.com/riobotz_combot_tutorial.pdf)**
  (Meggiolaro, PUC-Rio) — the deep reference for the underlying engineering:
  drivetrain, spinner energy, bite, shaft and bearing design, materials.
- **[SPARC](https://sparc.tools/)** — the construction, match, and tournament
  rules most North-American events adopt.
- **[NHRL wiki](https://wiki.nhrl.io/)** and **[Bristol Bot Builders guides](https://bristolbotbuilders.com/guides/)** —
  event procedure, radio setup, and tech-inspection detail.
