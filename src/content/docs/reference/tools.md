Free web calculators for the design math on this wiki. Most are from
**[Ember](https://ember.level5robotics.com/)** by Level 5 Robotics. Always
sanity-check a calculator's output against a hand estimate before you cut metal.

## Part selection

- **[Drivetrain Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/drivetrain)** —
  Wheel speed, gear ratio, and drivetrain performance. See
  [Power, torque & gearing](/fundamentals/power-torque-gearing/).
- **[Weapon Tip Speed Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/weapon)** —
  Spinner RPM and tip speed. See [Weapon energy & gyro effects](/fundamentals/weapon-energy/).
- **[Robot Builder](https://ember.level5robotics.com/builder)** — Step-by-step
  design of a complete robot powertrain.

## Mechanical design

- **[Center Distance Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/center-distance)** —
  Center distances for gears, chains, and belts.
- **[Scale Factors](https://ember.level5robotics.com/scale-factors)** — Convert
  linear dimensions between weight classes. See [Weight budgeting](/fundamentals/weight-budgeting/).

## Electrical

- **[Battery Size Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/battery-size)** —
  Pack capacity from current draw, discharge rate, and runtime. See
  [LiPo batteries](/power/lipo-batteries/).
- **[Watt-Hour Equivalency](https://ember.level5robotics.com/watt-hour)** —
  Equivalent capacities when changing cell count or voltage.
- **[Motor Torque Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/torque)** _(beta)_ —
  Motor torque, output torque, and linear force from motor specs.

## Safety

- **[Audio Tach Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/tach)** — Estimate
  weapon tip speed from an audio recording to check rule compliance.

## Strategy

- **[Tournament Calculator](https://ember.level5robotics.com/tournament)** —
  Fight counts and RCL point distribution for a bracket.
