---
title: Rulesets
description: Where to find the combat robot rulesets that events are based on.
---

Most events adopt or adapt one of a few widely used rulesets. Always follow the
specific event's published rules, but these are the common starting points:

- **SPARC** (Standardized Practices for the Advancement of Robot Combat) — the
  widely referenced open ruleset in North America, covering weight classes,
  safety, and arena requirements.
- **National governing-body rules** — many countries maintain their own
  (e.g. for the UK, mainland Europe, Australia); search for the body in your region.
- **Event-specific supplements** — individual competitions publish deltas on top
  of a base ruleset (weapon energy caps, arena size, allowed materials).
